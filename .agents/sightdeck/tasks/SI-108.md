# OTP login code valid for 1 hour

One-time login codes (`purpose: login`, storefront and admin) expire after 1 hour. Password-setup codes (`set_password`) stay at 10 minutes.

Update the login email copy and `medusa/docs/CUSTOMER_AUTH.md` so they match the new lifetime.
