# Sharpen Populaire vakgebieden thumbs

## Goal

Category tile thumbnails on the homepage (and the matching VA Thuis grid) should look sharp at the 75×~90px strip size, including on retina.

## Why they are soft

`SanityImage` in `fill` mode always requests a **1200×675** landscape crop. The tile box is portrait (~75×87). That combination:

1. Crops portrait sources to a thin 16:9 strip (e.g. 183×103 from a 183×275 asset).
2. Upscales that strip to 1200×675 on the Sanity CDN.
3. Lets Next.js Image pick a tiny srcset width (`w=64` / `q=75`) and `object-cover` it into a taller box.

Several seeded Cloudinary originals are also small (~180–290px). We cannot invent pixels, but stopping the wrong crop/upscale is the main win.

## Approach

- For `fill` (and `contain`), do not force width+height crop. Use `w` + `fit=max` so aspect is preserved and Sanity does not upscale.
- Keep explicit width+height crop when both are passed.
- Auto-format + quality 80 by default; category thumbs use 90.
- Allowlist `[75, 80, 90]` in `next.config.js` (`images.qualities`) — Next 16 otherwise coerces everything to 75.
- Same `SanityImage` usage on `CategoriesBlock` and `VaThuisCategoryGrid`.

## Outcome

Verified on `/`: Sanity URLs have `fit=max`, no `rect`/`h`; Next optimizer uses `q=90`. Portrait tiles show the full subject instead of a landscape strip. Some seeded Cloudinary originals remain ~180px; those can only get sharper with larger Studio assets.
