# VA Thuis cart quantity is always one

## Goal

VA Thuis colleges can be ordered once per order. Hide cart −/+ quantity controls for those lines and keep quantity at 1 if the bundle is added again.

## Approach

- Cart extras expose `is_vathuis`.
- `CartItemRow` uses the same locked quantity display as gift-card purchases.
- `addVariantToCart` and Medusa `POST /store/carts/:id/line-items` do not increase an existing VA Thuis line.
