# Show full agenda titles

## Goal

Agenda occurrence titles must never be truncated with an ellipsis. Long names wrap onto extra lines instead.

## Why

`AgendaRow` uses `line-clamp-2` on small screens and `line-clamp-1` from `sm` up. Some browsers then cut the title with `…`.

## Approach

Remove the line-clamp classes so the title wraps fully. Location can stay truncated.
