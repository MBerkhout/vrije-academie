# Align homepage column card thumbnails with wrapped titles

## Goal

On homepage `productCards` columns, the left thumbnail should fill the full card height when the title wraps, so it lines up with the title and CTA bar.

## Why

The thumbnail is a fixed `88×88` square. A one-line title plus CTA fits that height. A two-line title (e.g. “Workshop Landschap schilderen als Hockney in 3 lessen”) makes the card taller, leaving a gap under the image.

## Approach

Keep a minimum `88px` square, but let the thumbnail stretch with the flex row (`self-stretch`) and `object-cover` the photo. Do not clamp the title on desktop — the image grows with the card.
