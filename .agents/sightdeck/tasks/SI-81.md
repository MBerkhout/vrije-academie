# Investigate VA Thuis not adding to cart on staging

## Goal

Find why Joline cannot add VA Thuis products to the shopping cart on staging (mobile and desktop).

## Source

Asana: [Winkelmandje heeft problemen met VAthuis én studiedag](https://app.asana.com/1/1201227469632723/project/1216380929305123/task/1218626219533438)

Comment at 2026-09-22 08:54 UTC (10:54 Amsterdam) by Joline Beekmans:

> Ik krijg op mobiel én desktop nu uberhaupt geen VAthuis meer in mijn winkelmandje

## Finding

Mobile add-to-cart failed with HTTP 500 at 10:52 because the cart shipping country is `be` and that tax region has `provider_id = null`. Desktop already had "Leukste VAthuis lezing3" in the cart from 10:47 (later checked out as order 17).

## Evidence

- Mobile IP `144.178.215.218`: `POST /store/carts/cart_01M2T68XG5NJAECQRWF6VTFHRD/line-items` → **500** at 10:52:20 and 10:52:28.
- Medusa log: `Unable to retrieve the tax provider with id: null` during `add-to-cart` → `refresh-cart-items` → `upsert-tax-lines`.
- That cart: empty, email `jolinebeekmans@outlook.com`, shipping **BE**.
- NL tax region has `tp_system`; the other **26 EU tax regions have no provider**.
- Desktop IP `144.178.215.221`: added `Leukste VAthuis lezing3` at 10:47:39 (200). No further line-item POST around 10:53–10:54. Cart shipping **NL**. Checked out as order 17 at 11:38.

## Follow-up

Set `provider_id = tp_system` on all EU tax regions (and make `seed:region` write it), otherwise any non-NL shipping address 500s on add-to-cart.
