# Keep list text beside bullets on mobile

## Goal

On mobile, a wrapped list item should keep the bullet on the left and all text in a column to its right — never sliding under the marker.

## Why

`PortableText` uses Tailwind `list-inside`. That puts the marker in the first line of content, so wrapped lines sit under the bullet.

## Approach

Use hanging indents: `list-outside` plus left padding on `ul`/`ol`, so markers stay in the gutter and copy wraps as a block.
