# Fix digitale cadeaubon totals x100

## Goal

A €50 digitale cadeaubon must show as €50 in cart, checkout, confirmation, and payment totals — not €5.000.

## Cause

`POST /store/gift-cards/add-to-cart` stored `unit_price` as cents (5000). Medusa cart/order amounts are major EUR, so Medusa treated that as €5.000. Line items were special-cased on the storefront; confirmation, GTM value, and Mollie use Medusa totals and multiplied by 100.

## Approach

- Pass `unit_price` as major EUR (`amountCents / 100`); keep `metadata.gift_card.amount_cents`.
- Convert gift-card purchase lines like other cart/order money (major → cents).
