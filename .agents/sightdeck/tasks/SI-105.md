# Mobile PDP session location on the date line

On mobile session cards in `PdpLocationTabs`, the venue (`location_name`) sits on its own line with `pl-5` under the city. Move that venue onto the date line and drop the extra indent. The city row keeps the delivery icon, which indents the city name.
