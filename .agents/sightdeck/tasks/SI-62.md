# PLP shows product that misses filters

`/ons-aanbod?city=amsterdam&day_part=ochtend&period_start=2026-09-01&period_end=2026-09-30#product-jaaropleiding-kunstgeschiedenis` lists Jaaropleiding kunstgeschiedenis even though it should not match all three session filters together.

A product must appear only when at least one future session is Amsterdam AND ochtend AND in the selected period.

## Finding

Live `/store/events` with these filters returns the product. Its sessions:

- 23 Sep 2026 14:00 Amsterdam — middag (fails dagdeel)
- 26 Jan 2027 10:00 Amsterdam — ochtend (fails periode)
- **23 Sep 2027 10:00 Amsterdam — ochtend** (matches if Sep means every year)
- 29 Sep 2026 20:00 online — avond

SI-58 made a month chip match that calendar month in every year, so the 2027 start is enough. The card date is that 2027 session.
