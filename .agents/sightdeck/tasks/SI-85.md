# Widen agenda title at tablet width

## Goal

At tablet / mid widths, the agenda title column should be much wider. Time sits above price until `xl`, then they sit in the current side-by-side pair.

## Why

From `sm` to `xl` the time (`w-40`) and price (`w-24`) columns sit in one row and starve the title. Long names wrap mid-word (`Guggenhei / m`).

## Approach

- Below `xl`: stack time over price, drop the fixed widths, tighten CTA padding.
- From `xl` up: keep the existing `w-40` / `w-24` pair.
- Wrap titles on word boundaries (`break-words` off).
