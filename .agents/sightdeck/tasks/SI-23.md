# Hide hero newsletter card

## Goal

Editors can hide the hero "Meld je aan" newsletter card from Sanity, without removing the slider or top panel.

## Why

The newsletter teaser is always rendered today. Some pages (or campaigns) want the hero without that signup area; the top panel should then fill the right column.

## Approach

- Add `newsletterEnabled` on `heroBlock` (Newsletter tab), default on, matching `topPanelCtaEnabled`.
- Treat unset as on so existing homepage heroes keep the card.
- Hide the Aanmeldlink field when the card is off.
- `HeroBlock` skips the card when `newsletterEnabled === false`; the top panel keeps `flex-1` so it fills the column.
