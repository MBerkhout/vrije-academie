# Order linked to wrong Salesforce account

Salesforce order `8019X00002MNH18QAH` is Medusa order 22 (`order_01M37P8R2DP65SCCTQ9QJD6YJC`). The order email is `mick+23sep@thedigitalimprover.nl`, but the cart still belonged to customer `cus_01KPKGNRR70MAFTXQ1RNT76VJM`, whose email is `mick@thedigitalimprover.nl` and whose Salesforce account is `001Mz00001AO7oUIAT`.

## Resolution / Ergebnis

The cart `cart_01KW7MHWVAYP5VXT9Y0BCACSZM` was created on 28 June 2026 for the `mick@` customer. Checkout wrote the new email onto that cart and did not transfer it to `cus_01M37NNBSAMD096BQY5NCVQX15`. The Salesforce push sets `AccountId` from the cart customer, so the order landed on the `mick@` person account.

Order 23 (`8019X00002MRDgDQAX`, cart created 19:54) is on the `mick+23sep` customer and account `0019X00002MR4UrQAL`.
