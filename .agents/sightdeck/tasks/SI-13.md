# Restore listing scroll position when returning from a product

## Goal

When a visitor scrolls a listing, opens a product, and uses the browser back button, they should land back on the same card — not at the top of the page.

## Approach

- Give listing cards a stable HTML `id` (`product-{handle}` / `agenda-{id}-{variant}`).
- Before navigating to the PDP, stamp that id on the listing URL hash so the history entry has an anchor.
- On return, scroll the anchored card into view. If it was loaded via infinite scroll, keep loading batches until the card exists.

## Scope

Ons aanbod, Agenda, and VA Thuis catalog (and other `PlpEventCard` surfaces that share the click path).
